# Website Animations & Effects — Overview

A plain-language guide to every animation and effect on the site, organized by page and section.

---

## Site-Wide (appears on every page)

- **Custom cursor** — the normal mouse pointer is replaced with a small gold dot that follows your mouse with a slight trailing delay. It grows into a ring whenever you hover over a button or link.
- **Scroll progress bar** — a thin gold line at the very top of the page fills left-to-right as you scroll down.
- **Back-to-top button** — appears in the corner after you scroll down a bit, with a circular progress ring around it that fills as you approach the top.
- **Smooth scrolling** — clicking a link to another part of the page glides you there instead of jumping instantly.
- **Navigation bar** — shrinks and gets a solid background once you start scrolling; hides itself when scrolling down and reappears when scrolling up. Menu items reveal a dropdown preview when hovered. On mobile, the menu icon morphs into an "X" and opens a full-screen menu.
- **Buttons & links** — most buttons subtly follow your cursor when you hover near them ("magnetic" pull), lift slightly, and shift color. Arrow icons nudge forward on hover.
- **Accessibility** — anyone with "reduce motion" turned on in their device settings will see calmer, near-instant versions of everything below.

## Home Page

### Hero (top banner)
- The background photo slowly zooms and drifts on its own (a classic "Ken Burns" effect), and also shifts gently as you move your mouse or scroll.
- A soft gold glow drifts in the background.
- Small feature icons gently bob up and down, and perk up (scale + tilt) when hovered.
- The heading text rolls into view letter-by-letter, like an old flip clock.
- Everything fades and rises into place when the page loads.

### Trust Strip (client logos/quotes ticker)
- A continuously scrolling ticker of client statements that pauses when you hover over it.
- A small gold dot between each statement gently pulses.

### Industries
- A rotating globe graphic with little glowing dots orbiting around it.
- Industry cards fade and scale into view as you scroll, and tilt in 3D toward your cursor when you hover them.
- The card centered in view gently "breathes" (grows and glows) to draw attention.
- Hovering a card sweeps a light shine across it and brings its photo from black-and-white into color.
- A soft glowing hexagon grid in the background lights up near your cursor as you move over it.

### Solutions
- Each row fades in from the side as you scroll.
- Clicking a row (on mobile) smoothly expands to reveal more detail.
- On desktop, the photo on the right crossfades to match whichever solution you're looking at.

### Why Choose Us
- The portrait photo drifts gently as you scroll (parallax).
- Small floating tags beside the photo bob up and down.
- The list of reasons cascades into view one after another as you scroll.
- The same glowing hexagon background as above.

### Tech Stack
- Soft glowing shapes drift slowly in the background, along with a faint moving grid.
- A thin gold line continuously sweeps across the top of each panel.
- Panels and technology chips fade and rise into view as you scroll, and lift slightly on hover.

### Selected Work (portfolio)
- A dashed ring slowly rotates around the featured artwork.
- As you scroll through each project, its photo crossfades in with a subtle 3D tilt that follows your cursor.
- A progress bar fills to show which project is currently active.
- A "scroll to explore" button gently bobs to invite scrolling.

### Final Call-to-Action
- A large glow pulses softly in the background.
- The company name scrolls by continuously as oversized outlined "ghost" text behind the content.
- Heading and button fade/rise into view.

### Footer
- The large "Ascending Software" wordmark is revealed with a gold wipe effect that sweeps in from left to right as you scroll down to it (and reverses if you scroll back up).
- Links and columns fade in with a slight stagger.

## Contact Page

### Contact Hero
- A small animated gold envelope with sparkling particles floating around it, gently swaying and subtly tilting to follow your cursor. (On devices that can't handle 3D graphics, a simpler flat animated illustration is shown instead — orbiting rings, pulsing nodes, and glowing signal lines converging on a central checkmark.)
- A soft vertical line near the bottom pulses to suggest scrolling further.
- The heading rolls into view letter-by-letter, same as the homepage.

### Contact Info
- A vertical line connecting the process steps fills in progressively as you scroll down through them.
- The "replies within 1 business day" status dot pulses with a soft ripple.
- Contact rows highlight and shift slightly on hover.

### Contact Form
- A soft glow pulses gently behind the form card.
- After submitting, the form smoothly fades out and a success message fades in, complete with a checkmark that draws itself stroke-by-stroke.
- Form fields use "floating labels" that smoothly move up and shrink once you start typing.
- Option chips lift on hover and show a checkmark that slides in once selected.
- A spinning loader appears on the button while the form is submitting.

### Contact FAQ
- Clicking a question smoothly expands to reveal the answer, with the arrow icon rotating.

---

## Summary

The site relies heavily on gentle, elegant motion — soft glows, slow drifting shapes, letter-by-letter text reveals, cursor-reactive tilting, and scroll-triggered fades — to feel premium and polished without being distracting. Every animation gracefully scales back or turns off for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
